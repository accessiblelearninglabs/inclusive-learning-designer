Learning Design

Built with the people it serves.

1. Understand the need first

Understand the need behind a request before designing, including when the answer is not training.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Needs analysis for organizational transformation, Groundwork, the method

Also linked to. 2. Design with instructors, 3. Align outcomes, assessment and curriculum, 5. Design the learning experience, 12. Move evidence into practice, 13. Coordinate programs and partners, 16. Contribute to research and funded projects

2. Design with instructors

Shape a workable plan for a course in a single consultation, drawing on the instructor’s knowledge of their subject and learners.

Status. Practised · resource not yet written.

Evidence. No public resource yet.

Also linked to. 1. Understand the need first, 6. Facilitate shared learning, 13. Coordinate programs and partners

3. Align outcomes, assessment and curriculum

Align outcomes, assessment and activities across a course or program through competency-based design, so each part supports what learners are working toward.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Curriculum mapping design, Learning design resources, Case study: a learning evaluation framework for a national research institution

Closely linked to. 4. Design assessment for integrity and AI, 9. Remove barriers from assessment

Also linked to. 1. Understand the need first, 13. Coordinate programs and partners

4. Design assessment for integrity and AI

Design assessment that stays meaningful when generative AI is available, with clear expectations for its use.

Status. Practised · resource not yet written.

Evidence. No public resource yet.

Closely linked to. 3. Align outcomes, assessment and curriculum

Also linked to. 9. Remove barriers from assessment

5. Design the learning experience

Co-design preparation, scenario-based practice and reflection with learners and communities, so adults learn through experience and carry it into practice.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Experiential learning infrastructure design, Case study: equitable experiential learning at scale

Closely linked to. 6. Facilitate shared learning

Also linked to. 1. Understand the need first

6. Facilitate shared learning

Design and facilitate workshops, learning communities and Training of Trainers, in person and online, where participants learn from one another.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Learning community design and facilitation

Closely linked to. 5. Design the learning experience

Also linked to. 2. Design with instructors, 12. Move evidence into practice

Access and Equity

The barrier is in the design.

7. Design for learner variability

Apply Universal Design for Learning across ability, skill level and connectivity: hold the goal steady, identify where the design creates barriers, and build in flexibility.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Designing for learner variability at scale, Universal Design for Learning resources

Closely linked to. 8. Hold design and accommodation together, 9. Remove barriers from assessment

Also linked to. 10. Develop accessible e-learning and media, 11. Support Indigenous-led and antiracist design, 15. Evaluate, refine and report

8. Hold design and accommodation together

Hold inclusive design and individual accommodation as complementary practices, in support of the advisors who make accommodation decisions.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. From accommodation to proactive inclusive design, Accessibility resources

Closely linked to. 7. Design for learner variability, 9. Remove barriers from assessment

9. Remove barriers from assessment

Separate what an assessment is meant to measure from barriers unrelated to it, so every learner can show what they know.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Eliminating construct-irrelevant barriers in assessment

Closely linked to. 3. Align outcomes, assessment and curriculum, 7. Design for learner variability, 8. Hold design and accommodation together

Also linked to. 4. Design assessment for integrity and AI

10. Develop accessible e-learning and media

Storyboard and build e-courses, interactive activities and media in authoring tools, to WCAG 2.2 AA and tested with keyboard and screen reader.

Status. Practised · resource in development.

Evidence. Screen-reader version of the accessibility map, Accessibility resources

Closely linked to. 14. Run learning platforms and live delivery

Also linked to. 7. Design for learner variability, 13. Coordinate programs and partners

11. Support Indigenous-led and antiracist design

Offer design craft to Indigenous-led education, under Indigenous direction, and design against racism in content, assessment and participation.

Status. Practised · resource not yet written.

Evidence. No public resource yet.

Also linked to. 7. Design for learner variability

Systems and Evidence

Carried into practice and measured.

12. Move evidence into practice

Design the systems that carry research and guidance into practice, with policy and public-sector partners, and use learning to support organizational change.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Research-to-practice learning systems, Change management through learning design, Case study: a province-wide transition to care-based service

Closely linked to. 13. Coordinate programs and partners

Also linked to. 1. Understand the need first, 6. Facilitate shared learning, 15. Evaluate, refine and report, 16. Contribute to research and funded projects

13. Coordinate programs and partners

Plan and coordinate programs across partners and vendors in agile cycles, from terms of reference and budgets to quality review, version control and learner communications.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Case study: equitable experiential learning at scale, Organizational learning design

Closely linked to. 12. Move evidence into practice, 14. Run learning platforms and live delivery

Also linked to. 1. Understand the need first, 2. Design with instructors, 3. Align outcomes, assessment and curriculum, 10. Develop accessible e-learning and media, 15. Evaluate, refine and report

14. Run learning platforms and live delivery

Administer learning platforms, from course sites and catalogues to enrolments, completion tracking and reporting, and support live sessions with recording and captioning.

Status. Practised · resource not yet written.

Evidence. No public resource yet.

Closely linked to. 10. Develop accessible e-learning and media, 13. Coordinate programs and partners

Also linked to. 15. Evaluate, refine and report, 16. Contribute to research and funded projects

15. Evaluate, refine and report

Evaluate whether learning achieved its outcomes and transferred to practice, and for which learners, and refine it using learner feedback and analytics.

Status. Published resource.

Evidence. Learning evaluation framework design, Case study: a learning evaluation framework for a national research institution

Closely linked to. 16. Contribute to research and funded projects

Also linked to. 7. Design for learner variability, 12. Move evidence into practice, 13. Coordinate programs and partners, 14. Run learning platforms and live delivery

16. Contribute to research and funded projects

Contribute to educational research and funded teaching projects, including ethics review, funding proposals and resource mobilisation.

Status. Practised · resource not yet written.

Evidence. No public resource yet.

Closely linked to. 15. Evaluate, refine and report
